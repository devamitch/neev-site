2. Full Specification of Services & Data Collection

Neev connects with authorized third-party and Google APIs strictly to deliver user-facing features that you explicitly configure. Below is an exhaustive breakdown of all services used, data accessed, purpose of use, and retention policies:

map

Google Maps Platform & Location Services

Geolocation, Maps Grounding, Places API

Data Accessed: Ephemeral device geolocation coordinates (latitude, longitude) with user permission, address search queries, and route destinations.
Purpose: Providing real-time navigation to pediatric clinics, family event venues, school commute coordination, and grounding AI Guardian recommendations with verified local facilities.
Retention: Ephemeral. Geolocation is processed in real time and is never tracked in the background or sold to advertising networks.

auto_awesome

Google AI & Gemini (Multimodal AI Guardian)

Gemini 2.5/3.1 Flash & Pro, Voice TTS/Audio, Vision

Data Accessed: User-submitted family guidance queries, voice recordings for hands-free elder conversation, and uploaded images for homework or cultural artifact translation.
Purpose: Powering the Neev AI Guardian for personalized parenting guidance, Sanskrit/Hindi cultural storytelling, developmental milestone advice, and family schedule summarization.
Enterprise Privacy Protection: User prompts and family data sent to Google Gemini are processed securely via enterprise API keys and are NEVER used to train generalized foundation models.

calendar_today

Google Calendar API

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar

Data Accessed: Calendar event titles, event descriptions, start and end dates/times, and participant attendees.
Purpose: Harmonizing household rituals, children's school exams, doctor appointments, and family celebrations into a unified daily rhythm across all family stewards.
Retention: Synced locally in client storage during active sessions; events remain on your Google Calendar and can be disconnected at any time.

task_alt

Google Tasks API

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks

Data Accessed: Task titles, due dates, completion statuses, and task list names.
Purpose: Synchronizing shared household to-do lists, grocery coordination, elder medicine reminders, and parenting responsibilities between Google Tasks and the Neev dashboard.
Retention: Retained only while your Google account remains connected to synchronize task completion state.

folder

Google Drive API

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly

Data Accessed: File metadata, filenames, and file content of user-selected family documents.
Purpose: Allowing users to safely link and reference vital family documents, birth certificates, vaccination cards, and cultural heirloom scans within the private Legacy Vault.
Retention: Neev only stores references and file IDs; original files remain securely in your Google Drive.

mail

Gmail Integration

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly

Data Accessed: Email message headers and message text of school announcements, circulars, and medical appointment confirmations.
Purpose: Extracting school schedules, fee deadlines, and pediatric confirmations into the family calendar so working parents never miss an important milestone.
Retention: Emails are analyzed ephemerally to create calendar entries and are never stored in plain text or shared externally.

photo_library

Google Photos API

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photoslibrary.readonly

Data Accessed: Photo thumbnails and image media items explicitly selected by the user.
Purpose: Assembling generational family timelines, cultural ritual albums, and child milestone memory journals.
Retention: Photo references only; files remain stored in the user's Google Photos account.

account_circle

Google Identity & Profile (OAuth 2.0)

openid, userinfo.profile, userinfo.email

Data Accessed: User's name, verified email address, Google unique account ID, and profile picture avatar.
Purpose: Authenticating user identity, managing multi-generational family memberships, and assigning stewardship roles (Mother, Father, Elder, Child).
Retention: Stored securely in our encrypted database until account deletion.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use)

Neev's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In accordance with the Limited Use requirements:

  • Zero Advertising: We never use or transfer Google user data to serve advertisements, targeted campaigns, retargeting pixels, or sell data to third-party data brokers.
  • Zero Model Training: We never use Google user data or private family communications to train, retrain, or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models (including foundation LLMs).
  • Strict Human Privacy: Human operators, developers, or contractors are strictly prohibited from reading or inspecting your Google user data, except where you provide explicit written consent for troubleshooting, when necessary for security audits, or when required by applicable law.
  • Direct User-Facing Purpose: All data retrieved through Google APIs is used strictly to provide visible, user-facing family coordination features within the Neev interface.

4. Infrastructure, Data Storage & Security Safeguards

Neev employs multi-layered cryptographic safeguards to protect family records against unauthorized access, leakage, or loss:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored database records and token caches are encrypted using AES-256 standards on Google Cloud Platform and Firebase enterprise infrastructure.
  • Token Vaulting & Automatic Expiry: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens are stored in isolated encrypted storage with automatic time-to-live (TTL) expiration. Tokens are never logged in console outputs or exposed in client bundles.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict Firestore security rules enforce that only verified members of your specific family sanctuary can read or write shared family records.

5. User Data Retention & Deletion Mechanisms

You have complete sovereignty over your personal and family data. We offer three immediate ways to revoke access or delete your records:

Method 1: In-App Service Disconnect

Open Neev ➔ Navigate to Settings ➔ Google Connected Services ➔ Click "Disconnect". This immediately purges all active OAuth access tokens and ceases all synchronization.

Method 2: Complete Account Erasure & Data Wipe

Click "Delete Sanctuary Account" under Settings, or submit a request via email to amit@devamit.co.in with the subject line "Request Data Erasure - Neev". We will permanently delete your account, family profiles, task logs, and cached tokens from all production databases within 30 days.

Method 3: Direct Revocation via Google Account Permissions

You can revoke Neev's access to your Google account at any moment directly from your Google Security management console: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Protection of Minors & Child Privacy (COPPA & DPDP Compliance)

Neev is built for multi-generational Indian families. Child profiles (milestones, immunization records, school schedules) are created and managed strictly by authorized adult parent or elder stewards. Neev does not permit children under 13 to independently create accounts or link personal Google accounts without parental supervision, in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).

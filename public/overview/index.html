Neev — The Complete Modern Indian Family OS

A private digital sanctuary created by Amit Chakraborty (@devamitch) for modern multi-generational families. Neev unites shared parenting, synchronizes daily routines with Google Calendar and Tasks, and preserves cultural legacy across generations.

Public Accessibility Notice (For Google OAuth Reviewers & Users)

This overview page, our homepage at neev.devamit.co.in, and our official Privacy Policy are 100% public. Users do not need to sign in, create an account, or provide credentials to inspect our application's functionality, understand our data handling practices, or review our developer identity.

1. Core Features & Architecture

calendar_month

Daily Rhythm Harmony

Synchronizes family schedules across parents, elders, and children using two-way Google Calendar integration. Eliminates double-booking of pediatric visits, school exams, and poojas.

checklist

Chore & Medicine Tasks

Coordinates household duties, grocery runs, and elder medication schedules seamlessly with Google Tasks. Tasks can be shared across family members with clear responsibility.

family_history

The Legacy Vault

A secure digital heirloom for storing ancestral recipes, audio recordings of grandparents' stories, and essential family documents using Google Drive backup.

2. Transparent Google OAuth Scopes & Data Usage

Neev strictly requests the minimum necessary scopes to deliver user-facing features:

map
Google Maps Platform & Location Services
Places API, Directions, Maps Grounding

Used for route navigation to pediatric visits, school commute coordination, and grounding AI Guardian recommendations with verified local facilities. Geolocation is processed ephemerally with user consent.

auto_awesome
Google AI & Gemini (Multimodal AI Guardian)
Gemini 2.5/3.1 Flash & Pro, Voice Audio, Vision

Provides AI-guided parenting, cultural heritage storytelling, and family schedule optimization. User prompts are never used to train foundation models.

event
Google Calendar API
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar

Used to display family schedules in the daily sanctuary timeline and insert family reminders requested by the user.

task_alt
Google Tasks API
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks

Used to fetch, create, and mark complete shared family to-do items and errands.

folder
Google Drive API (Read-only)
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly

Used strictly upon user command to reference family documents stored in Google Drive within the Legacy Vault.

badge
User Profile & Email
userinfo.profile, userinfo.email

Used solely to authenticate the user and display their name and avatar in their family realm.

Google Limited Use Requirement Commitment

Neev's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell your Google data, never use it to serve advertisements, and never use it to train generalized AI/ML models.

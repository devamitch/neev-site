# Neev Sanctuary: The Modern Indian Family OS & Digital Sanctuary

> Neev Sanctuary is a private digital sanctuary engineered specifically for multi-generational Indian families. It combines ancestral wisdom preservation, Google Workspace calendar and tasks synchronization, sacred family vaults, real-time offline-capable 3D spatial family radars, and collaborative parenting workflows.

## Core Value Proposition & Cultural Architecture

- **Official URL**: https://neevsanctuary.devamit.co.in
- **Ecosystem**: Amit Dev Labs (https://devamit.co.in)
- **Category**: Family Operating System, Ancestral Digital Heirloom & Privacy-First Coordination
- **Key Concepts**:
  - **Sanskriti & Heritage**: Secure, encrypted storytelling and oral history preservation for grandparents and grandchildren.
  - **Kutumb Radar**: 3D spatial nearby proximity tracking built with zero external telemetry and privacy fences.
  - **Ritual & Festival Engine**: Pan-Indian tithi and muhurat scheduling harmonized with modern school and office calendars.
  - **Family Document Vault**: Zero-knowledge AES-256 encrypted storage for land records, medical histories, insurance policies, and wills.
  - **Google Workspace Integration**: Google OAuth2, Calendar, Tasks, Drive, and Contacts API integration with strict adherence to the Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements.
